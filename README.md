Katutubo

Katutubo is a mobile marketplace app that promotes and supports local indigenous products and handmade crafts in the Philippines. The app helps local artisans showcase their work while allowing users to discover and purchase authentic cultural products easily.

Overview

Katutubo is an Android mobile application designed to promote and support indigenous products, handmade crafts, and local businesses in the Philippines. The app connects users with authentic Filipino cultural products while helping local artisans and communities gain more opportunities through digital technology.

Katutubo provides a simple and user-friendly shopping experience where users can browse products, view details, save favorites, track orders, and receive notifications about purchases and updates. The application aims to encourage people to support local craftsmanship while preserving Filipino culture and traditions through modern e-commerce.

About the App

Katutubo was created to help preserve Filipino culture by supporting local communities and indigenous creators. The app provides a platform where users can browse products, explore traditional crafts, and support local businesses directly from their mobile devices.
